Derived units and prefixes

Derived units are built from base units. Write them in index form, such as m s⁻¹ rather than m/s.

Making reasonable estimates

  • An estimate is a sensible order of magnitude, not an exact value.
  • Compare with something familiar, then round to one significant figure.
  • Check the unit and the power of ten. Is the answer plausible?

Worked example: a cyclist's kinetic energy

1

Estimate: cyclist and bike m ≈ 80 kg, speed v ≈ 5 m s⁻¹.

2

E = ½mv² = ½ × 80 × 5² = 1000 J.

3

Check: the unit is the joule and the size is plausible.

E ≈ 1 × 10³ J
